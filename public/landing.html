Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know prior to scheduling.

What if I do not have my exact birth time?
An exact birth time from a birth record is ideal. If your time is approximate within an hour, we can still read your major planetary periods and divisional charts through preliminary rectification.
How does the virtual consultation work?
Sessions take place via high-definition private Zoom video call or phone audio. You will receive an immediate calendar invitation with the meeting link.
Can I reschedule if my schedule changes?
Yes, appointments can be freely rescheduled up to 24 hours prior to the session through the link in your booking confirmation receipt.
